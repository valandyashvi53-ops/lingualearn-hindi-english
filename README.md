# 🌍 LinguaLearn – Learn Hindi & English

Learn Hindi and English with short, fun, and interactive lessons! 🇮🇳 🇬🇧

LinguaLearn is a beginner-friendly language learning platform designed to help users improve their Hindi and English skills through lessons, practice activities, achievements, and progress tracking.

## ✨ Features

* 🏠 **Home Dashboard** – View your learning progress and daily goals.
* 📚 **Interactive Lessons** – Learn Hindi and English step by step.
* 🗺️ **Learning Path** – Progress through different learning levels.
* 🎯 **Practice** – Improve your language skills with regular practice.
* 🏅 **Badges** – Earn achievements as you learn.
* 🏆 **Leaderboard** – Compare your progress with other learners.
* ⭐ **XP Points** – Earn experience points by completing lessons.
* 🔥 **Daily Streaks** – Track your learning consistency.
* 📊 **Progress Tracking** – Monitor completed lessons and learning goals.
* 🔊 **Language Support** – Learn Hindi and English with audio support.
* ⚙️ **Settings** – Customize your learning experience.

## 📖 Learning Path

1. ✅ Basics
2. ✅ Greetings
3. 🍚 Family & Food
4. 🔒 Everyday Life

Complete lessons to unlock more learning content and improve your language skills.
